About Us

Passionate Bakers, Proud Caterers

Nthabi's Bakery & Catering Services was born from a deep love of food and a desire to bring people together over exceptional meals. Based in Hammanskraal, Pretoria, we have spent over 8 years perfecting our craft and building a reputation for quality, reliability, and taste.

  • Sourced from the finest local and imported ingredients
  • Experienced team of bakers and professional chefs
  • Tailored menus to suit any event or dietary need
  • Punctual, professional and fully equipped service
  • Trusted by hundreds of satisfied clients across Gauteng
